# Dense per-game encoder scaling: no demonstrated full-pipeline gain

Training and CPU postprocessing completed on 2026-11-09. Results were
inspected on 2026-11-10. All six checkpoints, both validation reports, and
four per-game prediction archives were produced successfully.

| Historical 2,000-player cross-fit | Competition utility |
| --- | ---: |
| Original C1+C2 | 0.7170117549 |
| C1+C2 plus expanded d96 member | 0.7149547904 |

The paired delta is **-0.0020569645**, with a 95% player bootstrap interval
of **[-0.0072978687, +0.0030000000]**. Both fold deltas are negative:
-0.0037927234 and -0.0003212056. The fixed advancement gate fails. This
does not establish that additional training games are inherently harmful;
it provides no evidence to replace the current production pipeline with
this particular expanded member.

## Interpretation

The added training pool used 36,000 cached games from 2,000 balanced players
alongside the original 15,211 games. Each fold excluded its own 1,000 test
players before training, leaving 42,227 and 42,587 games. The existing
validation cohort fit fusion weights and C1; test labels did not fit them.
Original baseline replay matched the recorded 0.7170117549 score.

Validation-fitted fusion assigned the original own-side d96 nearly zero
weight (about 0.00002), while assigning the expanded member 0.805/0.771.
These are unconstrained-sum geometric-fusion weights, not percentages. This
shows validation preferred the expanded member, but its selection did not
translate into improved held-player utility. Positive fitted weight alone
is insufficient evidence of a gain.

Training changes both per-player game coverage and class mixture; the
confounding factors are documented in [the protocol](PLAN.md). The historical
cross-fit cohort has been examined in earlier experiments, so this result
is an exploratory screen, not a fresh independent confirmation or Public
leaderboard result. No additional coefficient or seed sweep was selected
from this outcome.

## Decision and artifacts

Keep the original C1+C2 production method and its user-reported Public score
of **0.7227214**. No new official submission was generated by this experiment.
